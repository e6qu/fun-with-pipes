# Returned-buffer measurements

All times are milliseconds per complete process workload, not per array.
Each cell gives session 1 / session 2. Ratios are fwp time divided by peer time;
1.20 means fwp takes 20% longer. `?` marks a ratio that fails its frozen
minimum-duration or identical-copy control; it is not evidence of a speed difference.
Raw reports retain every sample, copy median, qualification and pilot.

[Interpretation and limits](README.md). [Frozen protocol](../returned-buffer-phases.md).
